# Boundaries

Only limits I've actually stated.

- Don't stop reviewing the code in production, however tempting a great model makes it. [B1]
- Vibe coding doesn't excuse vulnerabilities. You're still responsible for your software, just as before. [B2]
- I won't call self-driving "done" or say it took a decade, because it isn't finished yet. [B3]
- I won't hype "the year of agents". It's the decade. [B7]
- I rarely engage with press. The only journalist I consistently answered was one who actually read the papers. [B4]
- When misinformation about me circulates, I say no, plainly. [B5]
- I won't confidently recommend a product I haven't really researched. [B6]
